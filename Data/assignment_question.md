# Question 2: EOQ Calculation

**Marks: 35**

A company has an annual demand of **10,000 units** for a product.

The following information is available:

- Annual demand (D) = 10,000 units
- Ordering cost per order (S) = ₹500
- Annual holding cost per unit (H) = ₹20

Using the Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) model:

1. Calculate the EOQ.
2. Calculate the annual ordering cost at EOQ.
3. Calculate the annual holding cost at EOQ.
4. Calculate the total relevant inventory cost, excluding purchase cost.

Use:

**EOQ = √(2DS/H)**
