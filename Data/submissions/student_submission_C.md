\## Question 3



I would not immediately choose either a completely centralized or completely regional network.



Instead, I recommend a hybrid network.



The company could retain the central warehouse as the main inventory pooling location while establishing smaller regional distribution facilities in locations with high demand and long delivery times.



This approach may provide some of the responsiveness benefits of regional warehouses without completely duplicating inventory across all three locations.



Transportation costs could decrease for high-demand regions, while the central warehouse could continue serving lower-demand regions. Inventory pooling at the central location could also reduce the total safety stock requirement compared with fully independent regional warehouses.



The disadvantage is that the hybrid model may be more complex to manage because the company would operate two levels of distribution.
