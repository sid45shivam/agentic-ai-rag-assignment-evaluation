\# Feedback Generation



\## Purpose



Convert a validated evaluation into useful student feedback.



\## Inputs



\- Validated evaluation

\- Student evidence

\- Rubric

\- Quality-control result



\## Instructions



Generate:



1\. Strengths.

2\. Areas for improvement.

3\. Question-specific observations.

4\. Actionable next steps.



Keep feedback specific and connected to the student's actual response.



\## Output



Return:



\- strengths

\- areas for improvement

\- question-specific feedback

\- recommended next steps



\## Guardrails



\- Use only validated evaluation evidence.

\- Do not invent strengths or weaknesses.

\- Do not contradict approved marks or faculty decisions.

\- Keep feedback constructive and understandable.
